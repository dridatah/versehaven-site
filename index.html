VerseHaven Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026
Last updated: 19 November 2026

The one-sentence version

Your words never leave your phone. VerseHaven has no servers, no
accounts, no analytics, and does not request internet access.

How the app works (anonymous, on-device)

What the app stores, all of it on your device:

What leaves your phone: nothing, unless you choose to share something
yourself through your phone's share sheet (a verse card or a journal
export). Where that goes is entirely your choice, handled by your phone,
not by us. If you tap a helpline on the support screen, your phone's own
dialer, messaging app or browser opens it; VerseHaven sends nothing.

Verse search runs entirely on this device. When you type what you're
going through, the matching happens in an on-device model. If the crisis
support screen appears, that decision was made on your phone.

No backups to the cloud. Android backup and device-to-device transfer
are disabled for VerseHaven, so app data is never copied to Google
backups or moved to another phone.

No third parties. No analytics, no advertising, no crash reporters
that see your content, and no internet permission in the app.

Deleting your data: Profile → "Delete everything" erases your journal,
moods, activity and settings from the device. There is no cloud copy to
delete, because none exists. Uninstalling the app also removes all of
its data.

Future optional features

A future version may offer optional online features. They will be
opt-in, explained before use, and this policy will be updated first.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and contains no features
that collect data from anyone, including children.
